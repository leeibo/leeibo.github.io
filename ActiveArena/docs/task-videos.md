# ActiveArena task video assets

The website video wall contains one frozen demonstration observer-camera clip for each of the 35 benchmark tasks. The `preview_video` field in `task-videos.json` points to a 320×266, 15 FPS, CRF 28 version designed for simultaneous muted playback in the gallery; the original task clip remains available via the `video` field. The clips are copied from the local sample bundle and are used for qualitative visualization only; they are not policy observations.
